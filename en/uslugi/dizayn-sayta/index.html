When a new site pays off

Six situations where redesign or a new website returns the money fastest.

Traffic but no enquiries

People arrive and leave. Usually the structure answers the wrong questions, or the next step is hidden below three screens of decoration.

Broken on phones

More than half of visits are mobile. If buttons are too small and text overflows, the desktop version does not matter.

Prices are a mystery

No numbers means no trust. Even a range with an explanation filters out the wrong enquiries and warms up the right ones.

Site looks older than the brand

You changed positioning, packaging and prices, but the site still shows the version of the company from five years ago.

Nobody can update it

Every small edit needs a developer. A design system and clean templates let your team add pages without breaking the layout.

Launching something new

A new product or market needs its own page with its own arguments, not a paragraph squeezed into the old homepage.

Questions before we start

The things clients ask most often, answered honestly.

How much does website design cost?

A landing page starts at 80,000 ₽, a multi-page website at 120,000 ₽, a redesign of an existing site at 60,000 ₽. The final quote depends on the number of unique screens, the amount of content and whether development is included.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes 2–3 weeks, a multi-page website 3–5 weeks. Roughly a third of that time goes into structure and content, which is why we ask for your materials early.

Do you also build the website, or design only?

Both. We can hand over layouts for your developer, or build the site ourselves on Tilda or as a custom front-end with our development partner. Build cost is quoted separately once the design is approved.

What if we do not have texts and photos yet?

We start from your brief and write the structure and headings ourselves, then mark exactly which photos and facts are needed. Design on placeholder text always breaks later, so we prefer to settle content first.

Will the site work on mobile?

Yes. Every screen is designed in a desktop and a mobile version, and we check tap targets, long headings and form behaviour before handoff.

How do we pay and what about the contract?

We work under a contract with 50% upfront and the balance on delivery of the final files. Payment comes with an official receipt.

Tell us about your project

A few lines about your business is enough — we reply with a scope, a timeline and a firm quote. If the project is not a fit, we will say so and point you elsewhere.
