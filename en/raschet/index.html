Questions about the estimate

How accurate is this estimate?

It is a realistic range based on our actual projects, not a teaser price. The final number is fixed in the contract after a short brief, and it normally lands inside the range shown here.

Why are prices in roubles?

We are based in Moscow and quote in roubles for every client. For international clients we can invoice and accept payment in another currency at the exchange rate on the invoice date.

What is included in the price?

Research and concepts, design work, two revision rounds, final files in all required formats and editable sources. Rights transfer to you after full payment.

How do payments work?

We work under a contract with 50% prepayment and the balance after approval. Payment comes with an official receipt.
