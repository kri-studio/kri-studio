About

Big-agency level — without agency markup

kri·studio is an independent design studio: no middle managers and no inflated estimates. You talk directly to the person who builds your project.

The studio was founded by Kristina — a graphic designer with 5 years of experience. Over that time we delivered 450+ designs for 30+ brands — from local makers to the Moscow Metro.

Behind the scenes — freelance, print shop experience and commercial work across many industries: cosmetics, coffee shops, kids’ products, beauty, marketplaces. That’s why we feel confident in identity, packaging and print-ready design.

When needed, we bring in trusted specialists — copywriters, illustrators, 3D artists. You get a project team, but pay for work — not for an agency office.

Approach

Our principles

  • Meaning comes first.We start with who we’re selling to and what we’re selling — then we design. No pretty-but-useless visuals.
  • Honest timelines and pricing.We share realistic timelines upfront and fix them in the contract. No surprise extra fees.
  • Design ready for real life.Layouts print well, scale well and work on real touchpoints — not only in a presentation.
  • All source files are yours.After payment, we deliver sources and rights — no hidden “source files fee”.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a logo cost?+

A logo at kri·studio starts at 25,000 RUB; timeline — from 4 days. The price includes concept development, refinement of the chosen option and delivery of all source files. The exact estimate is fixed in the contract before we start.

How much does a brand identity cost?+

Brand identity starts at 90,000 RUB; a full brand package (logo, identity, brand assets) — from 220,000 RUB. Scope and timeline are fixed in the contract before the project starts.

How much does packaging design cost?+

Packaging design starts at 60,000 RUB. We design with print requirements in mind and deliver fully print-ready files.

How much do Wildberries and Ozon product cards cost?+

Marketplace product card design (WB/Ozon) starts at 20,000 RUB. We create selling infographics that meet platform requirements: for example, after we redesigned its cards, the Mangonia brand reached the top of its category search results.

How much does a website cost?+

A website starts at 80,000 RUB: design and build, mobile-friendly. Overall project budgets at the studio range from 15,000 to 320,000 RUB depending on the task.

Why are you cheaper than an agency?+

You work directly with the designer — no middle managers and no office markup. The level is proven by experience: 5 years, 450+ works for 30+ brands, including the Moscow Metro.

What are the timelines?+

A logo takes from 4 days. Timelines for other tasks depend on scope and are fixed in the contract before we start; revisions are usually implemented within 24–48 hours.

Do you work under a contract?+

Yes. Before we start, we fix the scope, price and timeline in a contract. 50% prepayment, the rest after approval. Payments are official with a receipt (self-employed, NPD). Closing documents are available upon request.

Who owns the design rights?+

After full payment, exclusive rights transfer to you. We deliver source files and all required formats. We publish the work in our portfolio only with your approval.

How do revisions work?+

We sync with you at every stage — from moodboard to final — so revisions don’t pile up at the end. We usually implement edits within 24–48 hours. The revision scope is fixed in the contract before we start.

Do you work with companies?+

Yes. We sign a contract and provide a receipt — everything is official and transparent for accounting.

We’re in another city/country — is it a problem?+

Not at all: we work fully remotely — calls, Telegram, e-mail. We present designs on real mockups and deliver files via cloud. Many of our clients are from other cities and countries.

What do you need from us to start?+

A short brief (10–15 minutes) and a few references you like. Then we suggest the best approach, estimate and timeline — and start right after approval.

Let’s discuss your project?

Tell us about your task and timeline, share references.We reply within a day and suggest the best way to help.
